Acme Corp Contractor and Consultant Policy (Version 1.4)

Engagement Terms
Contractors are engaged for fixed terms of 3 to 12 months and are not permanent employees. Contracts may be extended once, for a maximum of 6 additional months. Contractors report to a named engagement manager and must sign the non-disclosure agreement before receiving system access.

Leave for Contractors
Contractors do not receive paid annual leave or sick leave. Unpaid leave of up to 8 days per contract term may be taken with engagement manager approval, given at least 7 working days in advance. Unused unpaid leave cannot be carried forward or paid out. Public holidays are unpaid unless the contract states otherwise.

Working Hours and Location
Contractors work 40 hours per week. Remote work is limited to 2 days per week, and contractors must be on site on Tuesdays and Wednesdays. Remote work from outside the country is not permitted for contractors.

Expenses
Contractors may claim travel expenses only if pre-approved in writing. Meals are not reimbursed. Hotel stays are capped at 3,500 rupees per night. Claims must be submitted within 15 days and are paid within 20 working days.

Payment and Invoicing
Invoices must be raised monthly and submitted by the 5th of the following month. Payment is made within 30 days of invoice approval. Late invoices are processed in the next payment cycle.

Access and Equipment
Contractors receive a temporary laptop that must be returned within 3 working days of contract end. Access is disabled at 6:00 PM on the last day of the contract.
