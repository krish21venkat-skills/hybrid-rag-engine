Acme Corp Leave Rules (Effective January 2026)

Annual Leave
Every permanent employee receives 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month of service. New joiners who start after the 15th of a month begin accruing from the following month. Employees may carry forward a maximum of 10 unused annual leave days into the next year. Any balance above 10 days lapses on 31 March of the following year.

Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. Sick leave cannot be carried forward or encashed. A medical certificate from a registered practitioner is required if the absence exceeds 2 consecutive working days. Employees must inform their manager by 10:00 AM on the first day of absence.

Maternity and Paternity Leave
Maternity leave is 26 weeks of paid leave for the first two children and 12 weeks for the third child onwards. Paternity leave is 10 working days, to be taken within 6 months of the child's birth. Both can be combined with unpaid leave after approval from HR.

Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except for sick or emergency leave. Requests longer than 10 consecutive days require approval from the department head in addition to the line manager. Unapproved absence of more than 3 days is treated as abandonment of duty and may lead to disciplinary action.

Public Holidays
The company observes 12 public holidays each year. The holiday list is published every December on the HR portal. If a public holiday falls on a weekend, no compensatory day off is given.
