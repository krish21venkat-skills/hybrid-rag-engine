# IT Helpdesk FAQ

## VPN and Network
**How do I connect to the company VPN?** Install the Acme Secure Connect client from the software portal, sign in with your company email, and approve the multi-factor prompt on your phone. The VPN disconnects automatically after 12 hours of continuous use, and you must reconnect.

**Why does the VPN show error VPN-707?** This means your device certificate has expired. Certificates are valid for 1 year. Open a ticket with the IT Security desk to have a new certificate issued, which usually takes 1 business day.

## Accounts and Passwords
**How do I reset my password?** Use the self-service reset page and verify with your registered mobile number. Accounts are locked for 30 minutes after 5 failed sign-in attempts. If you cannot reset it yourself, call the helpdesk on extension 4400.

**How long does a new account take?** New employee accounts are created 2 working days before the joining date. Contractor accounts need an approved access form and take 3 working days.

## Hardware
**My laptop is slow or the battery drains quickly. What should I do?** Restart first, then run the built-in diagnostic tool from the Start menu. Battery replacements are free for laptops under 3 years old. Laptops are refreshed every 4 years.

**How do I request a monitor or keyboard?** Raise a request on the helpdesk portal under the category Peripherals. Approved requests are delivered within 5 working days.

## Support Hours
The helpdesk is open Monday to Saturday from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM IST. Critical outages are handled 24 hours a day through the on-call line on extension 4411.
